What this model does not include

  • Health cost is premiums only — no deductibles, copays or other out-of-pocket spending.
  • Immigration status, assets, and anyone aged 65 or over are not inputs.
  • Take-up is assumed for what is switched on above, and only that.
  • Annualised current-rule scenarios, not prorated calendar-year benefit totals.

For the client — Colorado, one parent, two children

You are paid $38,000 a year. You keep $67,000. More pay does not add to that until you are paid about $86,000.

The biggest drop is at $55,000 of pay: child care help ends and you keep $9,390 less. Food help ends at $54,000.

Your kids’ health plan ends at $72,000 of pay — but not that year. It ends at their next yearly check, up to 12 months later.
