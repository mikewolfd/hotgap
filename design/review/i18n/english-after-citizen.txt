If your pay goes up, do you keep more?

You are paid $30,000 a year. You keep $42,830. More pay does not add to that until you are paid $43,000: a raise of $13,000.

It happens again from $106,000 to $119,000.

You keep more than your pay because help and tax money are part of it. $1,142 of this is free school meals. It is not cash.

Above $64,500 a year, you can no longer apply for help with heating bills in California. It is called LIHEAP. It is worth $283 to $594 a winter if you get it. About 1 in 10 families who could get it here do. If you get it, turn it on to see it in your line.

Cash help ends. It is called TANF cash assistance.

$37,000

Food help would end. It is called SNAP. Some goes on until $38,000: $413 of food help.

Your own free state health plan would end. It is called Medicaid. Then help paying for health insurance would start. It is called the Premium tax credit.

One change waits

At $73,000 a year your kids stop being able to get a free state health plan. But the law lets kids keep it for a full year at a time. So it ends at their next yearly check, up to 12 months later.

We do not draw it as a drop today, because it is not one.
